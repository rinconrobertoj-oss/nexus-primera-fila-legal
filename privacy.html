1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how NEXUS Primera Fila, a personal desktop application, is intended to process TikTok account information for content analytics and editorial planning.

3. Information Accessed

Subject to authorization and API availability, the application may access:

  • Account identifiers, display name and avatar.
  • Profile information and biography.
  • Follower, following, like and video counts.
  • Public videos and available engagement metrics.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens required to maintain the authorized connection.

4. Purposes of Processing

Information is used to authenticate the authorized account, analyze content performance, generate reports, identify editorial opportunities and support AI-assisted content planning.

5. Legal Basis

Personal data is processed on applicable lawful grounds under data protection legislation, including consent where required. The TikTok account holder initiates authorization through TikTok OAuth.

6. Storage and Security

NEXUS Primera Fila is designed to use a local desktop integration. OAuth credentials and retrieved information must be protected against unauthorized access. Specific security measures and storage arrangements will be documented before production use.

7. AI Services and Data Sharing

Authorized account information may be processed through AI services used for performance analysis and editorial planning. External providers, information shared with them and applicable safeguards will be documented before such processing begins.

Personal data is not intended to be sold to advertisers.

8. Retention and Deletion

Information will be retained only as necessary for the stated purposes, subject to applicable legal and platform requirements. Specific retention periods will be established before production use.

To request access, correction or deletion, contact: rinconrobertoj@gmail.com.

9. Account Authorization

The account holder may revoke the application's access through TikTok's available authorization controls. Previously retrieved data may require separate deletion.

10. Data Protection Rights

Where applicable, individuals may exercise their rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. They may also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority.
